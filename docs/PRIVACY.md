# Privacy Policy — Replicant303

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Replicant303 ("the app") is an offline analog bassline synthesizer and sequencer for Android developed by DanimanX. This privacy policy describes how the application handles your information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. It operates entirely offline and does not request internet access.

## What the app accesses

- **Audio output** — to play real-time synthesizer audio through your device speakers or headphones. No microphone access is requested, and no audio is ever recorded.
- **USB MIDI devices** — to receive MIDI notes and clock from external controllers, and optionally send MIDI notes/clock out to external hardware when connected.
- **Local files you choose** — when exporting or importing standard MIDI patterns (.mid) or presets via the system file picker. Files remain entirely local to your device and are never transmitted over a network.
- **Foreground service & notifications** — to keep the audio engine and MIDI clock running reliably when the screen is locked or while switching between apps.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, accounts, device identifiers, or personal data.

## Data collection and sharing

- No user accounts or registrations exist.
- No third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising platforms, or crash trackers are bundled.
- The app does not declare the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission and cannot send or receive data over the internet.
- No user data is sold, tracked, or shared with third parties.

## Children's privacy

Replicant303 is a music production tool intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or anyone else.

## Changes to this policy

If future updates introduce new features that affect data handling, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated prior to release.
